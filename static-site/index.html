U.ART DIRECTION / UX / GRAPHIC DESIGN

DESIGNER'S EYE / DEVELOPER'S MIND

Visual thinking backed by the ability to build.

I’m Muhammad Usama, based in Karachi, Pakistan. My interests sit between graphic design, interface design, motion and creative technology. I studied graphic design at Aptech SFC, where I worked with Photoshop, Illustrator, Figma, Canva and After Effects. I continue developing that craft through independent visual studies.

I also have professional experience as a full-stack developer with 5Sum Ltd, working remotely on the UK restaurant SaaS platform Tummly. I built responsive React interfaces and ASP.NET Core functionality covering account onboarding, email verification and permission-controlled user flows.

That combination shapes my process. I care about distinctive composition, strong typography and thoughtful details—but I also understand responsiveness, usability, performance and the realities of implementation.
